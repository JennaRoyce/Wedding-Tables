## Layout Assumptions

Recommended working layout:

- 24 to 25 guest tables total
- 1 sweetheart table for Jenna and Tyler
- 1 clearly protected central dance floor
- 1 main circulation aisle from ballroom entry to dance floor
- 1 secondary aisle to buffet, bar, or service points

How to choose the table count:

- 23 tables would average almost 10 guests per table with very little flexibility for late RSVPs or awkward relationship splits.
- 24 guest tables works if the coordinator wants to preserve a larger dance floor and you are comfortable mixing some social groups.
- 25 guest tables is the safer choice if you want more breathing room for plus-ones, kids, and sensitive family splits while keeping major groups adjacent.
